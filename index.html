Frequently Asked Questions
How do I deposit money?+
Go to Wallet → Deposit, enter your amount, and tap Pay via M-Pesa. An STK push is sent to your phone. Enter your PIN and funds credited instantly. Minimum KSh 100.
How quickly can I withdraw?+
Withdrawals processed within 30 seconds, 24/7. No fees from SportyBet.
What is Cash Out?+
Cash Out lets you settle a live bet before the match ends. Lock in profit before the final whistle.
What markets are available?+
1X2, Double Chance, BTTS, Over/Under (1.5/2.5/3.5), Half Time Result, Draw No Bet, Correct Score, Total Goals Bands, Asian Handicap. Use Bet Builder to combine any markets.
How does Bet Builder work?+
Combine multiple markets from the same match — like Man City Win + Over 2.5 Goals + Haaland to Score — into one bet with a bigger combined odd.
Minimum bet?+
Minimum stake is KSh 50 per bet on website and WhatsApp.
Is SportyBet licensed?+
SportyBet is licensed and regulated by the relevant gaming regulatory authority.
